# heihei达人工作台

Chinese creator operations workbench for TikTok Shop Spain. The user requested independent username/password login with administrator and read-only accounts. The Site ID is kept in `.openai/hosting.json`; reuse it for future updates. The public entry page is protected by application authentication; every data endpoint must continue to authorize requests server-side.

## Main workflows

- Import Excel/CSV orders, sample sheets, SKU–PID mappings, Video Analysis exports, creator profiles, creator preference references, and monthly promotion sheets.
- Choose a worksheet and header row, review suggested field mappings, and confirm the report month/date range before merging.
- Reuse confirmed column mappings by normalized header name. Reordered columns are remapped; a missing or ambiguous previously selected header is left unmapped for review. An explicitly skipped field stays skipped. Import previews are tied to their source, field mappings, options and data revision.
- Samples are joined on order + platform SKU ID. SKU ID is preferred for product matching; Seller SKU only resolves when unique. Unresolved records remain reviewable.
- Video sales use explicit reporting periods, not publication month or malformed filenames. Same-period imports update sales measurements. Different, nonoverlapping periods add together, so a video's 500 units in one month and 10 in the next become 510. Overlapping periods require an explicit replacement decision; a new interval must cover any interval it replaces.
- Reports with unconfirmed dates can be staged in `pendingVideoImports`. Their video links may enrich matching sample records, but their metrics never enter cumulative sales or grades. The import center requires actual start/end dates, previews existing intervals that will be replaced, and archives those records in `videoReportArchives` before activation. A historical baseline and its archived details must never both contribute to lifetime totals.
- An explicitly confirmed historical report may instead become a `videoBaselines` snapshot with a known cutoff date and an unknown start date. Per the user's historical-data policy, select the highest `itemsSold` row for each video/PID across incoming historical rows, covered old monthly rows, and earlier baselines, then sum those per-video results. Keep the winning row's other metrics together and retain source provenance. Covered monthly reports remain archived references. Period increments must start after the baseline cutoff. Never invent a start date from video publication dates or distribute baseline sales into months.
- Video imports support a mapped `出单日期` column independently of `Post date`. Full dates identify daily increments; full year/month values identify monthly increments; a value such as `10月` uses the visibly selected import year. Row-supplied sales periods take priority over the file-level fallback. Missing years are never taken from publication dates. The preview must expose the actual periods before import.
- Published video counts use Video Analysis publication dates independently of sales periods. Repeated appearances across current reports, historical baselines, archives and active unconfirmed reports are deduplicated by video ID; per-product counts deduplicate within each PID. A sample match, positive sales, or a confirmed sales period is not required for a publication count. Missing publication dates remain in all-time counts and are never assigned to a month from sample dates or sales periods. Missing product mapping does not erase a known video from the global count. A June video selling in October counts as a June publication with October sales.
- Video views are lifetime observed maxima per video ID across valid source snapshots, including historical losing rows, then summed across different videos. Monthly view totals use the publication-month cohort, so a later higher snapshot updates its original publication month's maximum-view total. The `videoViewMaxima` collection retains accepted peaks across lower updates and period replacements; it does not resurrect removed publication records. Pending snapshots contribute only while valid and are persisted only when accepted. Sample-linked views deduplicate linked video IDs. Creator preference-table latest-view snapshots remain independent.
- The browser computes a SHA-256 fingerprint of each imported file. Repeat-file checks are scoped to the worksheet, while same-period video/PID keys update instead of adding duplicates. A video's publication date and identical units alone do not establish a repeat across independently confirmed new periods; a genuine October increment remains additive even if its units happen to equal September's.
- Missing PID mapping rows and sample rows do not delete existing records. Hand-edited fields are protected unless explicitly overwritten in the import options.
- Monthly promotion aggregates products by PID with a selectable representative SKU. Historical promotion values are references when detailed inputs are unavailable; unknown-period creator totals never add to monthly video metrics.
- Monthly promotion supports numeric ascending/descending sorting by sample orders, sample quantity, sample creators, published videos, and video-attributed units. Sorting applies to all filtered rows before pagination; page exports follow that order. The overview's product leaders remain independently ordered by sales.
- Missing old-month performance is displayed from saved sources, without inventing actual sales dates: confirmed monthly reports (including zero) take priority, then explicit historical promotion sales, then active baseline sales grouped by canonical publication month for months at or before the baseline cutoff. Reference cards, product rows, charts and exports identify the source and cutoff. `stats.itemsSold` and creator lifetime sales remain confirmed totals; `displayItemsSold` supplies the labeled historical view. All-month totals must never add these monthly references again. Months after the cutoff wait for actual new-period reports.
- The managed creator list combines sample-ledger creators with existing manually maintained creator profiles. Creators without a sample record are also included once their confirmed cumulative sales exceed an editable threshold (10 units initially), and are labeled as admitted by sales. Preference-only prospects and below-threshold video accounts do not automatically enlarge the list. Recorded collaboration count is effective sample quantity: two samples count as two collaborations, including two units in one sample row. Cancelled and refunded samples do not count.
- When a sales-qualified creator has no sample records, the user's self-purchase policy supplies an explicitly labeled default of one sample/cooperation. The default is editable, and each creator's purchased sample quantity can be entered separately, including a deliberate zero. Do not infer purchased quantity from the number of videos or products. Once sample records are supplied, actual effective ledger quantity replaces this fallback rather than adding it again; the entered purchase value stays available for review.
- Creator rules are ordered and editable. Any creator who does not meet another enabled rule defaults to L1. Creator views are the latest explicitly supplied preference-table snapshot, with missing data shown separately from zero. Video-report views do not fill that field.
- Creator product contributions show cumulative sales, share of the creator's sales, the leading video, and whether the product matches an actual sample. A successful sample video has more than 10 cumulative units by default; its threshold is editable. Missing product/sample matches remain visible instead of being invented.
- Cooperation willingness is explicitly maintained, with configurable choices initially set to `未沟通`, `高`, `低`, and `随性`, and `未沟通` as the default for blank profiles. Changing the available choices or default never rewrites existing nonblank labels. Administrators can select a page, individual creators, or all matching filtered creators and save one batch update; ordinary accounts remain read-only. Batch updates validate the entire managed selection, preserve notes and other manual fields, update duplicate raw profiles consistently, and protect the chosen willingness against subsequent imports. A deliberate choice of the default is also stored as a manual value.
- Excel exports keep historical hand-entered metrics separate from calculated video metrics so an export/edit/reimport cycle preserves the original reference values. The complete workbook includes monthly promotion sheets across all available months, independent of the current page filter.
- The admin-only `管理员中心` lists login accounts and supports creation, role changes, suspension/reactivation, password reset and deletion. New accounts default to read-only; account mutations never change creator business data or enter its undo/backup history. Current administrators cannot delete, suspend or demote themselves, and server-side writes preserve at least one active administrator.
- After an Excel file is generated, the page keeps an explicit download link. File generation is not reported as a completed browser download.
- The in-app `使用说明` explains metric scopes, deduplication, historical fallback, percentages and examples, with current saved thresholds and grade rules. It distinguishes sample-link progress (matched effective rows / all ledger rows), creator product sales share (product / that creator's confirmed lifetime sales), and overview chart length (relative to the leading product row). Keep guide copy aligned with the calculation engine when changing business rules.
- Sample, promotion and creator tables provide clickable page numbers in groups of 20 (1–20, 21–40, etc.), group jumps and first/last-page shortcuts, while retaining 12 records per page. Filtering and shrinking totals keep the current page within range.

## Data and services

`lib/creator-engine.mjs` is the pure import, merge, matching, aggregation and grading engine. `lib/video-periods.mjs` validates staged-report periods and previews/archives replacements. `lib/import-presets.mjs` restores column selections without relying on old column positions. `lib/creator-willingness.mjs` validates willingness settings and applies atomic, manual-field-preserving batch updates. `components/creator-workbench.tsx` is the client UI; `components/workbench-guide.tsx` provides the usage and calculation reference, and `components/workbench-admin.tsx` provides account management. The vendored SheetJS browser library reads Excel; `nodim:true` handles a known damaged sheet dimension in the original Video Analysis file. Long identifiers remain text. TikTok date text is MDY; import auto-detection also handles Excel serial dates and explicit formats.

`GET /api/state` returns `{state, revision, updatedAt, canUndo}` to authenticated accounts. `PUT /api/state` accepts `{state, revision, reason}` with optimistic concurrency; `POST /api/undo` accepts `{revision}` and restores the previous saved snapshot. Both mutations require an administrator session. Ordinary accounts may view and export only. Cross-origin mutations are rejected. The platform service credential is not an application-authentication bypass.

`GET /api/auth/me` returns the current account or `null`. `POST /api/auth/login` verifies the password and creates a server-side session; `POST /api/auth/logout` revokes that session. Session cookies are HttpOnly, Secure in production, and SameSite=Lax. D1 stores opaque-token hashes and expiration, with separate login rate limits for account and IP. Roles and active status are resolved from the current database account, never from browser-supplied claims. Account changes revoke existing sessions; the browser revalidates on window focus and every minute while signed in.

Production configuration uses two secret environment entries: `WORKBENCH_AUTH_ACCOUNTS` and `WORKBENCH_SESSION_SECRET`. The initial account entry is an array of `{id, username, role, salt, hash}`; roles are `admin` and `viewer`. An atomic, one-time bootstrap copies these existing password hashes into the D1 account tables without changing the supplied passwords. After initialization, D1 is the authority for accounts; environment changes do not overwrite accounts or recreate deleted entries. Salt and hash use base64url. Password verification uses native scrypt with N=16384, r=8, p=5, 32-byte output and independent random 16-byte salts. Never put plaintext passwords, production hashes, session tokens, or runtime secret values in source, client bundles, backups, or logs. Manage accounts through the authenticated administrator endpoints. A completed account change takes effect without another deployment. Secret rotation still uses native Sites configuration and a new deployment. Never remove the initialization marker or use environment bootstrap as an automatic account recovery path.

`GET /api/admin/accounts` requires an administrator and returns public account metadata. `POST /api/admin/accounts` creates an account; `PATCH /api/admin/accounts/[id]` changes role, active status or password; `DELETE /api/admin/accounts/[id]` removes a login account. All mutations require same-origin JSON, and responses never expose password hashes or salts. New and reset passwords must contain 8–256 characters; existing short passwords remain compatible. Account management is separate from `/api/state`, so restoring a business backup cannot reinstate a deleted account.

Persistent state is chunked into D1 records, with a current revision, previous snapshot and bounded history. The maximum accepted state is 24 MB. Database changes are schema-only Drizzle migrations; user data must never be embedded into migrations, static assets, or the Git source.

The initial four user-provided workbooks are imported into the runtime database separately from source publication. Future updates must read the current cloud state before merging so manual notes and mappings are preserved. Never replace cloud state with an old initial seed.

## Development and verification

Use the Sites skill's official setup, build, preview and source workflow helpers. Do not publish with a separate deployment system. The production Site is always the project recorded in `.openai/hosting.json`.

Run `node tests/auth.test.mjs` for authentication, one-time account bootstrap, account CRUD, session revocation and concurrent-administrator safeguards; `node tests/creator-engine.test.mjs` for business invariants and export/reimport regressions, `node tests/video-periods.test.mjs` for explicit-period activation and archival behavior, `node tests/video-publications.test.mjs` for publication-date counts independent of sales periods, `node tests/video-views.test.mjs` for per-video peak views and accepted/pending snapshot behavior, `node tests/historical-month-display.test.mjs` for historical display source priority and unchanged confirmed totals, `node tests/import-presets.test.mjs` for column-reordering and ambiguity handling, `node tests/creator-willingness.test.mjs` for batch-update and manual-field integrity, and `node node_modules/typescript/bin/tsc --noEmit --incremental false` for types. Use the Sites build helper to emit deployable Worker assets. Local D1 and the hosted D1 are separate databases.

Default grade rules: any creator with at least 60 units → L5; at least 2 samples and 51+ units → L5; 20–50 → L4; 11–19 → L3; exactly 10 → L2; below 10 → L1. The L4/L3/L2 rules require at least 2 samples. All unmatched cases are L1. These defaults are editable in the UI; unknown-period historical profile totals remain references rather than additional sales.
